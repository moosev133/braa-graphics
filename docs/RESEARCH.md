## Original direction

Cream editorial canvas; plum typography; blush and lavender chapter surfaces; restrained berry action buttons and yellow strokes. Hebrew-first hierarchy with a few quiet English studio labels. A large product still life, alternating rounded category silhouettes, tactile gift photography and paper-inspired compositions create the boutique character.

No competitor descriptions, code, proprietary artwork or layouts were copied. No client work, reviews, business history, prices, stock or delivery promises were invented. All product photography is clearly labeled as illustrative. The actual logo and introduction-post image were absent from the supplied attachment, so the wordmark is explicitly provisional.
